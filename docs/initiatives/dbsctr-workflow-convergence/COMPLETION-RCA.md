## 7. Model, reviewer and orchestration recommendations

Use one primary and serial shared-file ownership. No model switch, parallel agent
budget or new reviewer service is justified by this evidence. Independently review
critical provenance/custody changes under existing project authority. No Scout
research is needed for local contract contradictions. Helper access failure must
be reported without bypass or claims of completed delegated work.

## 8. Guardrails and rejected shortcuts

Reject removing only the amendment guard, weakening required gates, equating merge
with acceptance, rewriting recorded commits/baseline, manually editing records,
forcing worktree deletion, treating retired as completed or reusing evidence with
unknown inputs. Preserve original failures, exceptions, private custody, native
permissions, source-publication checks and independent destructive approval.

## 9. Delivery slices and success measurement

Core owns proof and affected reconciliation; journey consumes it; rollout installs
and qualifies one nominated recovery. See COMPLETION-RECONCILIATION.md for profiles,
plans, dependencies and exact ownership. Baseline: current synthetic refusal plus
source-backed unsupported composition. Candidate procedure: disposable public-CLI
journeys and negative adversarial cases, then qualified installed first-consumer
proof. Threshold: all supported composite journeys complete or retain an actionable
missing-proof state; zero forged gate credit, lost evidence or cleanup side effects.
Count required executions versus retained applicability in fixtures; do not project
whole-session speedups. Residual risk: unavailable legacy input identity can require
real revalidation; ambiguous provenance must remain blocked with a supported path.

Smallest independently deliverable P0/P1 slice: core provenance reconciliation,
amendment composition and structural preflight together. A guard-only patch leaves
the topology dead end intact. Implementation requires fresh Discovery receipt,
Build preflight and exact approval.
