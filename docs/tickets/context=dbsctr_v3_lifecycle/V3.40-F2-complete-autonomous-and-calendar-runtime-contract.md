## Outcome

Operators receive separate truthful autonomous-runtime and calendar-runtime
aggregates without raw OpenCode scans, private span disclosure, or weakened gates.

## Context

Build correctly reopened `V3.40-F1` because the approved response omitted the
required calendar aggregate and the Phase Span interface could not identify
Excluded Pauses. The user approved explicit pause operations and separate
calendar mean, p50, and p90.

## Scope

Add `operator_wait` and `external_wait` Phase Span operations and implement the
source-local `cycle-performance` JSON report. Reuse Cycle Records and the private
phase ledger while preserving existing record and review compatibility.

Do not modify or retire prior worktrees, add automatic OpenCode hooks, federation,
DKS repair, execution concurrency, provider tuning, hosted services, or gate
reductions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Complete non-pause helper timing produces interval-union autonomous runtime.
- Explicit operator and external waits are excluded and may not overlap active
  intervals.
- Missing, incomplete, ambiguous, or overlapping timing remains unavailable and
  does not enter autonomous aggregates.
- Reports include separate autonomous and calendar integer mean and nearest-rank
  p50/p90, coverage, unavailable counts, and quality counters.
- Filters are validated and source-local; empty populations are truthful.
- The command never reads `opencode.db`, creates state during a read, or emits
  cycle IDs, ownership paths, detailed spans, or sensitive content.
- Existing records remain readable without migration.
